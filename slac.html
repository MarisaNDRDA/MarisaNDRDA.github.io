The full story

As a controls intern, I led the HCI-focused redesign of SCORE, the software that saves, loads, and activates the configurations the particle accelerator runs on through EPICS. I interviewed accelerator control-room engineers and operators, built several prototypes, narrowed them down with their feedback, and built the final UI in Python/PyQt to control-system standards. It is intended for use across the national laboratories. I also worked through the database changes needed to save data the way the control-room staff wanted it stored.

I also converted hundreds of legacy EDM operator screens (Java and C) to Python/PyDM. These are the screens that show control-room operators the live status of equipment on the beamline, and I kept the existing EPICS control backend intact.

As a software engineer, I built a locally hosted prototype of a new proposal-submission portal (Django, React, PostgreSQL, Docker) that showed lab leadership the overhaul was feasible. It restarted a modernization effort that had stalled for years. I then benchmarked two peer laboratories’ proposal systems on self-hosted headless Linux servers, cataloged feature gaps, and gathered requirements from four beamline user facilities for a universal proposal system. I’m now finalizing that plan for lab directors.
