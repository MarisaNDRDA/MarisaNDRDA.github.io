The full story

Each week I turned the teacher’s challenge into a working robot and lesson. I assembled the kits, wrote and tested the C/C++ code for Arduino and Raspberry Pi, and walked students through it. One example is a BLE-controlled, line-following rover with manual and autonomous modes. I also used MATLAB to graph robot behavior.
